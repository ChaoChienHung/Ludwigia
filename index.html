About Me

Right now, my main focus is on machine learning and deep learning, alongside software development and distributed systems. Lately, I've also been diving into networking and cloud architecture. It might sound like I'm trying to take on too much, but to borrow Steve Jobs' words: "Stay hungry, stay foolish." For me, it's simply about chasing what I genuinely love.

The tech world feels like an endless treasure hunt. There's always a new problem to solve or a fresh perspective to discover. Of course, the sheer amount of information can be overwhelming at times, but that feeling when you finally grasp a tough concept or fix a complex bug? That is exactly why I keep falling in love with this field over and over again.

Beyond tech, I'm deeply curious about a variety of subjects, including biology, physics, history, and current affairs. I really enjoy the collision of ideas across different disciplines. It often provides a completely new lens through which to understand the world.

Outside of work, my lifestyle is pretty dynamic. I love watching baseball and esports, playing basketball, and staying active through fitness and swimming. I'm also a big fan of anime, TV series, and gaming. On top of that, I'm planning to challenge myself with drawing soon, and I'm looking to pick up the piano again after letting it slide for a while.

If you're also curious about the world or feel like we're on the same wavelength, definitely feel free to reach out. I'd love to connect!
